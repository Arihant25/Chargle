The three typefaces used on this site are served from here rather than from a
font CDN, so visiting the page does not tell anyone else that you were here.

Anybody, by Eli Heuer and contributors.
Variable, used for headings. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anybody

Martian Mono, by Evil Martians and Roman Shamin.
Variable, used for labels and readouts. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Martian+Mono

Author, by Indian Type Foundry.
Used for body text. ITF Free Font Licence.
https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/author

Only the Latin subset of each is included.
